MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Understanding the Captioning Model

Image captioning combines two major deep learning components: an encoder that understands the visual content and a decoder that generates language.

✓

Visual Feature Extraction

The encoder transforms the image into a numerical feature representation.

✓

Language Generation

The decoder predicts words sequentially based on the visual features.

✓

Natural Caption

The predicted words are combined into a meaningful human-readable sentence.
